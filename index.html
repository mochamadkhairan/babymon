My Babymon,

Happy birthday to the person who somehow became one of the most important parts of my life.

I don't know if words will ever be enough to explain how grateful I am that I met you.

We've had our good days, our random days, our silly moments, and probably a few moments where we both wondered what the hell we were doing.

I'll always be grateful for every memory we've made, every laugh we've shared, and every ordinary day that somehow became special simply because it was with you.

So today isn't just about celebrating another year of your life.

It's also about celebrating the person you are, the person you're becoming, and the person I'm lucky enough to love.

Happy 34th birthday, Babymon.

I love you. More than I probably know how to put into words.
